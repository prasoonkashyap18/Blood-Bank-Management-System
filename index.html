Blood Bank & Donor Management

Connecting donors, requesters, and blood banks in one place

LifeDrop helps people find nearby blood donors, lets volunteers register to donate, and gives blood banks a simple dashboard to track inventory and requests. This prototype demonstrates the complete user experience with sample data.

How LifeDrop works

1. Register as a donor

Create a donor profile with your blood group and city so people searching for blood can find you.

2. Search or request blood

Browse the donor directory or submit a blood request with urgency and location details.

3. Blood banks coordinate

Administrators track inventory, review requests, and monitor supply from a single dashboard.

One donation can save up to three lives

Join the LifeDrop donor directory in a few minutes. No commitment required beyond registering your interest.
